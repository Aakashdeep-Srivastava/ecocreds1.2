# CarbonGero — Product Strategy (real-world, people-first)

> Who actually uses this, why they'd care, what comparable apps do, and the
> features that genuinely move the needle. Grounded in competitor research
> (Nov 2026). Anti-vanity: every feature below traces to a real job-to-be-done.

---

## 1. The honest competitive landscape

| Player | What it does well | What it's missing |
|---|---|---|
| **Cool The Globe** (India) | 100+ simple actions, country-based targets, log → see avoided CO₂, a **"Global Meter"** of collective impact, org/team competitions, buy credits + sponsor trees | Actions are **self-reported** (no evidence/verification); no tradable asset; no SMB accounting |
| **Klima / Wren** | Footprint quiz → **offset subscription** funding vetted projects, education | Offset-only; you don't *earn* anything; no proof your own actions count |
| **Joro / Commons** | **Auto** footprint from bank/card spend, nudges | Measurement-only; passive; no marketplace |
| **Earth Hero** | Action library + **habit tracking** + education + community | No offsets, no B2B |
| **Greenly / Ecologi (biz)** | SMB GHG Scopes 1–3, SBTi plans, supplier surveys, credit due-diligence, compliance reports | **Expensive** (Greenly ≈ $10k+/yr); Greenly has **no offset marketplace**; built for finance teams, not people |

**The gap nobody fills:** turning a *verified individual action* into an
*auditable, tradable asset* that an SMB can actually buy. Trackers measure;
offset apps sell someone else's credits; accounting tools serve CFOs. **CarbonGero's
wedge = Proof of Sustainability: evidence → AI + human verification → on-chain
proof → tradable credit → public marketplace.** That chain is our moat.

## 2. The real user journeys

### A. Aarti — a concerned individual with a cause
1. **Lands via the cause**, not the tech. Hero should answer "what can *I* do?" (Cool The Globe's founding question), not lead with "blockchain".
2. **60-second footprint calculator** → sees her number + the one or two actions that matter most for *her* (not 100 generic tips).
3. **Does a real action** (recycles, cycles, switches energy) → **uploads a photo** as evidence.
4. **AI gives instant feedback**; it goes to a human verifier; she gets a **Proof of Sustainability** + **CGRO points**.
5. **Sees collective impact** ("you + 4,120 people avoided 3.5M kg") — the single most motivating screen in these apps.
6. **Redeems points** for real rewards, **or** her verified impact becomes a micro-credit an SMB buys — *she earns, she doesn't just pay.*
7. **Learns** along the way: short explainers on what actually removes carbon (reforestation, biochar, DAC) so she trusts where money goes.

### B. Ravi — runs a 25-person manufacturing SMB
1. Needs ESG proof for a **larger customer's supply-chain survey** (the real trigger for SMB climate action).
2. Can't afford Greenly ($10k+). Wants **affordable, simple** measurement + a way to *act*.
3. **Measures** basic Scope 1–2 (energy, fuel) → gets an estimate + report.
4. **Engages his team**: employees log verified actions under the company → a **cooperative team meter** (not a punitive leaderboard — see §4).
5. **Buys verified local credits** on the marketplace (incl. ones generated by his own employees/community) → **retires** them → gets a **certificate + on-chain proof** for the customer survey.
6. **Books future offsets** — pre-commits to a monthly tonnage so he can forecast and budget.

## 4. Retention: design for the 12-week cliff
Research is blunt: ~**90% of users drop off a climate app within 12 weeks**, and
**punitive leaderboards hurt** retention while **cooperative milestones sustain
it**. So:
- Lead with a **shared goal meter** (team/community avoided-CO₂), not rank.
- **Streaks + gentle nudges**, not guilt.
- Make the **reward loop tangible** (points → real redemption / earnings), since
  "offset-only" apps have nothing to keep you coming back.
- Education as **progress** (unlock explainers), not a content dump.

---

## 5. "Teach people end-to-end" (carbon capture)
Most users don't know the difference between **avoiding** emissions and
**removing** them. A simple, trustworthy education layer:
- Plain-language explainers: **reforestation vs biochar vs direct air capture**,
  with real cost/permanence (biochar ≈ €100–210/t and already delivered at scale;
  DAC = highest-integrity, pricier). Registries like **Puro.earth / Isometric**
  set the credibility bar.
- **Project transparency** on every credit: what the project is, how it was
  verified, the on-chain proof — so buying feels informed, not blind.
- We already have a strong **SEO blog**; surface it *in the flow* (at the footprint
  result, at checkout), not just as a separate section.

---

## 6. How SMBs benefit (the business case)
- **Cheaper than incumbents** — serve the SMB that Greenly/Watershed price out.
- **Measure + act + prove in one place** — basic Scope 1–2, buy verified credits,
  retire, get a certificate for the customer survey that triggered it all.
- **Employee engagement as a feature** — a cooperative team meter; staff-logged
  verified actions can become credits the company retires (closes the loop).
- **Local credits** — buy offsets generated by verified actions in their own
  community, not opaque far-away projects.

---

## 7. "Book it for the future — get people involved now"
Capture intent before the full product is ready:
- **Pledge** — "I'll avoid X kg / plant Y trees / go car-free on weekends." Public,
  shareable, with a shared pledge meter.
- **Pre-commit / subscription** — individuals or SMBs reserve a monthly offset
  tonnage (forecastable, recurring); fulfilled from the marketplace.
- **Early-access waitlist** — sign up by cause/role (individual / SMB / verifier /
  project developer) → segmented launch + a real, growing user list today.

---

## 8. Recommended build order (each ties to a real job)
1. **"Get Involved" — pledge + waitlist** (public, no auth): captures real people
   now, matches "book for future", smallest lift. *← start here*
2. **Footprint calculator + collective Global Meter**: the universal on-ramp + the
   top retention screen.
3. **Education layer in-flow** (carbon-capture explainers on results/checkout).
4. **Real auth + accounts** (Clerk): unlocks per-user impact, teams, SMB.
5. **SMB flow**: team meter + basic Scope 1–2 + buy/retire → certificate for surveys.
6. **Real AI verification** (FastAPI): anti-fraud before money scales.
