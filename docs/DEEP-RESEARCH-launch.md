## Executive summary — the 5 findings that matter most

1. **You cannot be your own accredited registry at launch.** ICVCM's Core Carbon
   Principles require a program to have been **operational ≥24 months**, with a
   published methodology, a registered project, and a public no-login registry,
   *before* it can even be assessed — and assessment then takes 6–8 months. A
   brand-new "CarbonGero registry" is, by definition, **voluntary and
   unaccredited**. Keep labelling it exactly that (we do).

2. **EU law now directly threatens the "Proof of Sustainability" + "carbon
   neutral" framing.** The Empowering Consumers Directive (2024/825), in force
   EU-wide from **27 Sep 2026**, **bans self-created sustainability badges/labels**
   not backed by third-party certification, and **bans "carbon neutral / net zero"
   claims based on offsets**. Enforcement is already live (TotalEnergies fined;
   Apple barred from "carbon neutral" Watch in Germany). Penalties **≥4% of
   turnover**. → Our naming and any "neutral/offset" marketing is a real risk.

3. **The reward-token model is probably OK on securities, but money-movement is
   the real burden.** Tokens **earned** (not bought) likely fail the Howey "investment
   of money" prong → less likely to be securities; the SEC's Mar-2026 framework
   treats **issuer profit promises** as the trigger (so never promise CGRO will
   appreciate). But the moment you move real money / allow cash-out, you hit
   **state-by-state money-transmitter licensing (~$1M+ all-in) + FinCEN MSB + KYC/AML**,
   or EU **MiCA CASP / EMI**. → Use a **licensed partner**, don't DIY.

4. **Demand for *high-integrity* credits is real and growing — but the integrity
   bar is far above photo+AI.** 90% of corporate buyers plan to keep/grow buying;
   nature-based prices cluster **$15–30/t**; **removals command a ~381% premium**
   over avoidance. Yet high-integrity supply is scarce (<10% of projects get
   top ratings; one study found **<16%** of ~1B credits were genuinely additional),
   and credible credits need **defined baseline, third-party MRV, additionality
   tests** — a bar *"recycling photo + AI"* does not clear. → Our evidence→credit
   pipeline, as built, yields **voluntary proof tokens, not registry-grade carbon
   credits.** Position accordingly.

5. **The 12-week retention cliff is confirmed — and social features are the gap we
   can own.** In a controlled test, Earth Hero users "would have abandoned after
   ~2 weeks" without reminders; Wren reported subscriber churn and a ~6% revenue
   dip; across the 8 top footprint apps, **community/social features are
   underdeveloped**. → Our cooperative **Global Meter + teams + earn-don't-just-pay**
   loop targets exactly the proven weakness.

## 1. Legal & regulatory (the launch blockers)

**Carbon-credit integrity / registry accreditation**
- ICVCM CCP = the "minimum common denominator" for high integrity; a **two-step**
  assessment of *both* program and methodology, CCP label only if both pass. [ICVCM]
- To be assessed, a program must be a legal entity with **≥1 approved public
  methodology, ≥1 registered project, an operational public registry (no login),
  finalised public procedures, and ≥24 months of continuous operation.** [ICVCM]
- Non-CORSIA entrants must meet **all 176** Assessment-Framework requirements, plus
  multi-decadal sustainability, buffer-pool/wind-down plans, COI policies, and
  **their own AML + anti-bribery** processes covering the registry and any KYC
  providers. Assessment takes **6–8 months**. [ICVCM]
- **Implication:** CarbonGero credits are voluntary/unaccredited at launch. Partner
  with or sell *recognised* credits (Verra/Gold Standard/Puro/Isometric) for the
  "real credit" leg; keep CGRO proofs as a distinct, honestly-labelled voluntary tier.

**Greenwashing / environmental-claims law**
- EmpCo Directive **2024/825** in force EU-wide **27 Sep 2026** (the Green Claims
  Directive proposal was *withdrawn* Jun 2025, but EmpCo's substantive bans
  remain). Transposition deadline **27 Mar 2026**. [ecoclaim, McDermott/Italy]
- **Banned:** generic "eco-friendly/sustainable/green" without on-medium
  certification; **offset-based** "carbon/climate/CO₂ neutral / net zero"; and
  **self-created sustainability badges** not based on third-party certification. [ecoclaim]
- Penalties **≥4% of annual turnover** (≥€2M if turnover unknown); national maxima
  e.g. Italy up to €10M, France up to €100k or 80% of ad spend, Germany €250k +
  profit disgorgement. Active enforcement: TotalEnergies (€10k/day), Apple Germany. [governancedocs, ecoclaim]
- **Implication:** audit CarbonGero's copy. Avoid implying our badge = certification
  or that buying credits makes a company "carbon neutral". The app's existing
  "voluntary-market / estimated / not accredited" labels are the right instinct —
  enforce them everywhere, including marketing.

**Payments, KYC/AML & securities**
- US money transmitters need a **license in each state** (fees $500–$5,000+, surety
  bonds $10k–$1M, net-worth $100k–$500k); **~$1M+ all-in**; plus **FinCEN MSB (Form
  107, renew every 2 yrs)**, SARs within 30 days, 5-yr record retention, KYC. [innreg]
- EU: crypto services → **MiCA CASP** (passportable once authorised); holding/moving
  fiat or issuing e-money → separate **EMI/PI**; single-currency stablecoins =
  e-money tokens (credit/EMI issuers only). [thesignal/MiCA]
- SEC (Mar 17 2026 framework): five categories, three explicitly **non-securities**;
  a non-security can *become* a security via **issuer profit promises/milestones**;
  **airdrops without bargained-for consideration aren't securities**; **tokens
  earned via spending fail Howey's first prong.** [WilmerHale, crushrewards]
- **Advice from sources:** partner with a licensed provider to test markets / for
  non-core features (live in weeks); get your own license only when the regulated
  activity is core (many months). [thesignal]

## 2. Voluntary carbon market — demand, pricing, where micro-credits fit

- **Demand is holding; the market is in transition.** 2024 retirements steady at
  **182 Mt** (top-10 standards) even as transaction volume fell **25%** to a
  post-2018 low; prices down only **5.5%**; market value at **1.9× 2018**. [Ecosystem Marketplace SOVCM 2025]
- **Quality premium is the signal:** removals carried a **381% premium** over
  avoidance (up from 245%); recent-vintage credits **+217%**; CCP-approved
  categories jumped (Landfill Gas volumes +149%, prices +35%). [Ecosystem Marketplace]
- **High-integrity is scarce & supply-constrained:** BBB+ credits >⅓ of retirements
  (up from ~25% in 2019); **<10% of 4,000+ projects get AAA–A**; ~**40% mismatch**
  between high-integrity supply and demand. [MSCI, Sylvera]
- **Corporate demand is durable but narrow:** 90% of buyers plan to continue/grow;
  common asks **$15–30/t** (nature-based); buyers expect only **~7%** of their
  abatement from removals/offsets (two-thirds from own operations). [Morgan Stanley survey, n=225 >$1B firms]
- **The integrity risk for us:** a landmark analysis found **<16%** of ~1B credits
  were additional; additionality (barrier/investment/common-practice tests) and
  rigorous MRV are gating. [10insightsclimate]
- **REFUTED (0/3 survived):** the claim that *"low-quality avoidance dominates →
  weak demand for high-integrity micro-credits."* Evidence points the other way —
  demand concentrates on **quality**. Don't plan around "cheap sells".
- **Implication:** there's a real, premium market for *credible* credits — which our
  "recycling-photo micro-credit" is **not** without serious MRV. Two honest paths:
  (a) sell CGRO as a **voluntary proof/impact token** (not a tonne-of-CO₂ claim), or
  (b) aggregate verified actions under a **recognised methodology + third-party MRV**
  to earn real credits. Don't blur the two.

## 3. Competitors & business models

- **Wren** (subscription offset): routes **70%** of funds to projects, keeps 30%;
  **self-funded**, no outside investment; but contributions **−6% YoY** on churn and
  "few cost-effective growth opportunities"; notes climate attention is **declining**.
  A candid signal that consumer-offset subscriptions are hard to grow. [Wren report]
- **Ecologi — Climate Action Workforce**: **headcount-based B2B2C subscription**
  (one monthly payment funds trees + certified projects per employee; in-app
  engagement layer). A proven SMB revenue model to copy. [Ecologi]
- **Deedster**: employee-engagement challenges in **20 languages**; pitch = engaged
  employees outperform. Localisation is table stakes for global B2B. [Deedster]
- **Earth Hero / academic test**: users **"would abandon after ~2 weeks"** without
  reminders; apps don't integrate into daily routines (solitary, reflective use);
  **social/community features underdeveloped across the top 8 apps**; self-selecting
  audiences hit an **engagement ceiling**. [edepot thesis]
- **REFUTED (0/3):** AWorld's "83% active, 270 actions/user/month" retention stats —
  too good, unverified. Treat vendor retention claims skeptically.
- **Implication:** our edge is (1) **cooperative social** (Global Meter, teams) where
  peers are weak, (2) **earn-not-just-pay** (offset apps have no return loop), and
  (3) **headcount B2B2C** as the SMB revenue engine.

## 4. Go-to-market (individuals + SMBs)

- **B2B2C via employers** is the most credible cheap-acquisition channel: one SMB
  contract onboards many individuals, and sustainability is pitched as an
  **engagement driver** (Ecologi/Deedster/Eevie all run this play).
- **Retention > acquisition**: given the 12-week cliff, cheap acquisition is wasted
  without cooperative streaks, reminders, and a tangible reward loop.
- **Localisation** (languages, country emission factors) is required for a global
  employee base.
- **Implication:** lead GTM with **SMB employee-engagement** (recurring revenue +
  bulk user onboarding), with the public funnel (calculator → learn → pledge) as
  the top-of-funnel for individuals.

## What to change in CarbonGero because of this

1. **Rename/reposition the token tier** away from anything implying certification or
   neutrality; "voluntary proof / impact token" — never "carbon-neutral". (Greenwashing law.)
2. **Never claim CGRO appreciates** or promise managerial profit. (Securities.)
3. **Don't custody/convert fiat** — integrate a **licensed ramp/payment partner**;
   keep KYC with them. (Money transmission.)
4. **Separate two product tiers cleanly:** voluntary proof tokens (ours) vs
   recognised carbon credits (Verra/Gold Standard/Puro/Isometric, resold) — don't
   present a recycling photo as a tonne of CO₂.
5. **Build the cooperative/social + reward loop** hard — it's the demonstrated
   retention gap.
6. **Make SMB employee-engagement (headcount subscription) the revenue engine.**
